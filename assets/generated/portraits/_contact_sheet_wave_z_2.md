# Wave Z.2 — Dramgid NPC Portraits

All sixteen physical designs in this batch are **canon interpolations**. The NPC roles, districts, and factions are canonical, but no vault physical descriptions were available; age, gender presentation, build, features, clothing details, and visible work implements were inferred for distinctness, role readability, and mundane plausibility.

Shared portrait direction: 512×512 neutral bust; semi-realistic painterly dark-fantasy rendering; muted era-3 Dramgid charcoal, blue-black, damp brown, dark iron, pale bone, and restrained tarnished-bronze palette; directional upper-left light; plain dark atmospheric background; ordinary weathered working people; no text, border, magic, glowing eyes, armor spikes, or heroic posing.
