## table--long

Source: `/home/adamjroder/.codex/generated_images/01a09bcb-b822-78d3-9a31-565bc87f4a97/exec-4e530274-a6a5-4739-8753-0bb337019f62.png`

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: transparent 256×256 interior prop sprite for the Soul Meter 2D isometric CRPG
Primary request: Generate one long tavern table / sturdy service table, replacing a light generic asset-kit table while preserving a compact readable silhouette and practical game footprint.
Input images: Image 1 is a style reference for Soul Meter world props: assets/generated/sprites/world/dom-crate-wood--stacked.png. Image 2 is a style reference for Soul Meter world props: assets/generated/sprites/world/dom-hanging-lantern--decorative.png.
Subject: table--long, a single elongated dark timber table with thick worn plank top, inset iron brackets, subtle tarnished bronze fasteners, slightly irregular hand-built construction; no chairs, stools, dishes, bottles, cloth, people, or extra props.
Style/medium: gothic mythopunk. Semi-realistic painterly digital illustration — closer to dark-
fantasy concept art than to flat vector/pixel art or a clean 3D-kit render. Rich surface
detail (wear, rust, moss, wet sheen) rendered through paint-like shading rather than flat
color fills or hard cel outlines.
Lighting/mood: dramatic, directional key light with a darker falloff/vignette around the
subject. Avoid flat, evenly-lit "asset kit" lighting — every piece should look like it
was lit for atmosphere, not for orthographic clarity alone.
Color palette: Base surfaces: charcoal/near-black stone, blue-black masonry, wet dark iron.
Institutional metal: iron and tarnished bronze, reserved for ceremonial/important
elements (see the brazier's warm firelight accent against cold stone).
Magic/the Wound: restrained violet, cyan, pale bone-white — used as small local
accents, never an all-over glow.
Organic life: deep moss/algae green, damp peat brown, occasional demon-heat red-orange
(see Bog Wight's corrupted growth).
Overall: desaturated and dark by default, with a small number of deliberate saturated
accents per asset (fire, magic, decay) rather than broad bright color.
Composition/framing: Isometric single-subject framing, top-down/isometric camera, one object only, transparent background, bottom-center ground contact, no text, no UI, no watermark, no outline stroke. Centered compact sprite; full object visible; transparent PNG with real alpha.
Materials/textures: scarred dark wood, damp grain, worn edges, oxidized iron hardware, restrained bronze glint.
Constraints: preserve a readable long-table silhouette and compact footprint; no checkerboard painted into the image; no background plane; no extra objects.
```
