# Wave X Combat Backdrops

All four images are **PROVISIONAL owner-review surfaces**. Each is a 1672×941 painterly combat backdrop composed for an overlaid isometric battle grid: upper-left directional light, a muted era-3 Dramgid palette, receding environmental depth, and a relatively calm lower play area. No text, characters, UI, or baked-in gameplay grid is included.

- `bog-marsh-battlefield-v1.png` — Loamroot bog with dark shallow marsh water, drowned roots, sodden tussocks, wet peat, and low bone-grey mist; desaturated moss greens and damp browns frame an open central fighting plane.
- `jawbrace-ledge-battlefield-v1.png` — The Wound Lip's first ledge: fractured damp charcoal stone above a black abyss, distant broken shelves, and restrained wet-iron braces and chains; cold blue-grey light and deep falloff reinforce oppressive stillness.
- `trial-hall-battlefield-v1.png` — Registry trial hall with severe carved pillars receding into gloom, cold light from high slits, and a broad worn dark-stone floor kept visually quiet for combat readability.
- `wound-touched-field-battlefield-v1.png` — Neutral era-3 field subtly made wrong by ash-dusted grey-green grass, tired earth, a faint pallor, low broken field boundaries, and distant trees leaning at unnatural angles.
