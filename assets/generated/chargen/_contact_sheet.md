## Canon interpolation log

- **Vael:** no physical-description gap to fill with a fantasy trait: canon explicitly says their alteration is temperamental rather than gross-anatomical and that they read as the baseline people. The restrained choice was to add nothing.
- **Vaerin:** canon gives no outward physical description. Both portraits and the panel use ordinary human-readable morphology and distinguish the ancestry through Pozor craft, records, age range, and clothing only.
- **Kaan:** part-mineral, stone-fused physiology is explicit, but mineral type, placement, and surface pattern are not. Granite/slate-like patches were used quietly; crystals, runes, lava fissures, and glow were excluded.
- **Weftkin:** cultivated tissue and mycelial integration are explicit, but the normal visible surface anatomy of an ungrafted individual is not. Fine fibrous texture and pale hyphal branching were used; loud mushroom or body-horror signatures were excluded.
- **Kes'reth (Mirror-Veil):** horns, mirror-bodied physiology, reflective function, and paired scars are explicit. The intensity and material appearance of the reflective body are not, so the images use subdued living sheen rather than literal chrome.

## Shared art direction

- Semi-realistic painterly dark-fantasy illustration with paint-like material shading.
- Desaturated charcoal, blue-black, damp brown, moss, pale bone, and restrained tarnished bronze.
- Rich wear, grime, wet stone, cloth, leather, mineral, fungal, glass, and metal texture without pure-black outlines.
- Era-3 travel/craft dress; no high-fantasy armor clichés, steampunk dominance, cartoon proportions, broad magical glow, or baked text.
