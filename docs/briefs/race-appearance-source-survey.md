# Race appearance — historical source survey

**Prepared:** 2026-09-10, before the author's seed-lineage correction. **Status:** historical
research record, superseded for design by [Part III of the Art Aesthetics Bible](../art-aesthetics-bible.md#part-iii--seed-race-design-guidelines).

**Use Part III to design races.** The author clarified that this work is a coalescing
text guideline for their own 3D modeling and Blender captures. Seed races come first;
the 20-file inventory below is not the seed roster. The author confirms **nine seed
races**, with **Solan and Vael naming the same people**. Fiel are monkey–turtle
humanoids; Ghorr are four-armed and almost ursine with warthog-like facial cues;
Mirror-Veil is dropped. Several founding-seed assignments and material identities in
the older sources below are also superseded. The original ten-question questionnaire
is retired, including its asset-production and Mirror-Veil assumptions.

## Sources and authority

The requested `/dramgid-bible/` path was not present locally. The matching repository is [`dramgidian-bible`](../../../dramgidian-bible/README.md). Its original [Race Bibles](../../../dramgidian-bible/_source/03_Race_Bibles.md) describe seven races. Its [instructions](../../../dramgidian-bible/AGENTS.md) defer to [`dramgid-vault`](../../../dramgid-vault/AGENTS.md). That repository precedence does not override the author's subsequent explicit corrections recorded in Part III.

At survey time the [vault manifest](../../../dramgid-vault/index.json) contained **20 peoples entries**, including Dragons and the combined Giants & Goliaths entry. This is a repository inventory, not the author's seed-race classification. Chapter One's older [five playable ancestries](../phase-0-ratification.md#6-ancestries-d4--the-five-with-kesreth-as-new-canon) are implementation/design history, not the scope of the current aesthetic guideline.

The older repository's [review handoff](../../../dramgidian-bible/docs/review-handoff.md) preserves author decisions awaiting reconciliation. The tables below retain the source comparison as it stood before the latest author correction. Within those tables, “current” means the vault snapshot used for that comparison, not current design authority:

- **Vault snapshot:** described by the vault at survey time, with its provisional wording preserved.
- **Historical candidate:** visual detail found in the older Bible; retained for review rather than silently imported.
- **Character-specific direction:** an existing game art brief, not evidence that every member of that race looks the same.
- **Open:** an asset-making decision these sources do not adequately specify. A fantasy analogue is not a complete anatomy specification.

Shimari, Orthos, Fiel, Ghorr, and Kes'reth rows particularly carefully: the author's
new account changes their inheritance, appearance, or both. Mirror-Veil references
below record what was found, not a branch to continue designing.

## Historical Bible: appearance details retained for review

All seven entries below come from the older [Race Bibles](../../../dramgidian-bible/_source/03_Race_Bibles.md). Names are mapped to the current roster for comparison, not to declare every historical claim an alias or current fact. Historical lifespans are recorded as provenance, not approved age targets.

| Historical entry → current comparison | Description available in the older Bible | Import boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Humans → Vael | Ordinary human bodies, wide variation and adaptability; historical lifespan 70–90 years. | Broad human appearance is compatible with the current baseline description. The old survivor-stock origin is not the current seed account. |
| Ashborn → Kes'reth | Horns; ash-toned through deep-red skin; eyes that hold light; heat-adapted bodies. Historical lifespan 90–120 years. | Skin palette and eye treatment are useful candidates, not blanket approval for both modern branches. The current source treats caldera survival as an in-world founding myth rather than factual origin. |
| Deepkin → Kaan | Short, dense bodies; fungal symbionts worn as living second skins; poor distance vision and strong dark vision. Historical lifespan 200–300 years. | Current Kaan physiology centers stone fusion. Do not replace it with obligatory fungal skins or make symbiont dependence canonical by importing this passage. |
| Vaerin → Vaerin | Formerly near-ageless, 500+ years; lifespans contracting as their Weft bond fails. | This provides aging context, not a face or body sheet. Current record-bound life is the governing detail. No ear length, skin palette or transparency is established here. |
| Ghorr → Ghorr | Big, powerful, resilient bodies; described as larger, stronger and faster-healing, with contamination resistance. Historical lifespan 60–80 years. | Useful provenance for build. The old origin account differs from current canon; color, tusks and facial anatomy remain unspecified. |
| Sporeborn → Weftkin | Humanoid bodies of interwoven fungus and salvaged organic matter; living fungus over mineral-and-bone frames; partial regrowth. | Fungal bodies are supported by the current entry. Exposed bone/mineral scaffolds, host growth and a particular mushroom-cap design are not automatically approved current visual defaults. |
| Dragons → Dragons | Enormous, powerful, effectively ageless individuals; the older account makes them engineered pre-Bloom survivors. | Size language is historical reference. **Do not import engineered pre-Bloom origin:** the current source explicitly states dragons did not exist before the Bloom. Neither account supplies a complete anatomical sheet. |

## Earlier game art references

The [Art Aesthetics Bible](../art-aesthetics-bible.md) already fixes gothic mythopunk, semi-realistic painterly rendering, readable silhouettes, worn materials, restrained magic accents, isometric single subjects, transparent **256×256** world-unit outputs and bottom-center ground contact. Race design should add subject identity within that style.

The [character art brief](../../art-request.md) requests **512×512** three-quarter bust portraits, and its older field-sheet section describes smaller native footprints and directional walk frames. These are legacy integration references. The author has now specified making 3D models and capturing them in Blender; this documentation task does not produce assets. Source canvas size is not the same as on-screen body height or animation footprint.

These character-specific descriptions record earlier art direction. Their race anatomy
does not override Part III, and the Serai-Lun row does not retain the dropped Mirror-Veil branch:

| Character | Existing art-brief direction | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Vex, Ash-Bound Kes'reth | Tall, dense build; swept living horns, layered ash cloth, repaired dark armor, cinder-ink. | Vex's silhouette and unusually held throat/sternum mark are not universal Kes'reth traits. |
| Serai-Lun, Mirror-Veil Kes'reth | Light duelist silhouette; mirror shard or polished veil over one eye; reflective plates and glass-like sash. | An eye covering is a character design, not proof of a universal racial organ or costume. |
| Old Grumbrand, Kaan | Heavy patched coat, salvage lens, riveted harness, tools and soot. | His kit does not settle Kaan mineral anatomy. |
| Wyneth Hallow-Tide, Ghorr | River healer, blue-gray coat, steady hands, tide-line mark and river-water vial. | The tide-line mark and healer's kit are personal, not race-wide markings. |
| Ressa Quickfingers, Vael | Cropped travel jacket, tools, keys, chipped tooth, alert posture. | An individual occupational design. |
| Korrath Ninefold, Orthos | Imposing build, ritual scars or metal studs, worn harness and gauntlet. | Studs are an optional brief treatment; they do not establish a metallic body. |
| Maura Greyfen, Snarlin | Weatherproof salvage kit; an old wound with a pale patch of **fur or skin**. | The brief itself leaves fur versus skin undecided. |

These are written reference directions. No existing image was visually evaluated or approved as a race-wide template during this survey. Iris Illepah and Marshal Coiljaw's race assignments are still marked provisional in the art brief; do not use those passages to decide a species design.
