# Provisional trust-language redlines

Status: **Pending human canon decision**

These are proposals only. No quest resource or dialogue prose has been changed. All three quests
become active when offered, and the follow-up question is immediately available when the dialogue
returns to its hub; there is no field-trust gate. The old wording therefore promises progression
the mechanics do not implement.

## Serai-Lun — The Mirror Held Steady

Quest description location: `quests/serai_lun_mirror_line.tres:9`

```diff
- Serai-Lun keeps her guard up even with the party. Earn enough of her trust in the field and she'll finally say why.
+ Serai-Lun keeps her guard up even with the party. Ask what line she is watching and why it matters.
```

Dialogue follow-up location: `dialogue/companions/serai_lun.dialogue:9`

```diff
- Serai-Lun: Ask again when you've held it a while. I'll know if you have.
+ Serai-Lun: Ask again if you mean to hear the answer. I don't give it twice.
```

## Wyneth Hallow-Tide — The Kept Name

Quest description location: `quests/wyneth_hallow_tide_kept_name.tres:9`

```diff
- Wyneth Hallow-Tide leaves one line blank on every muster form she fills out. Earn enough of her trust in the field and she'll finally say whose name it is.
+ Wyneth Hallow-Tide leaves one line blank on every muster form she fills out. Ask whose name she keeps off the muster-roll.
```

Dialogue follow-up location: `dialogue/companions/wyneth_hallow_tide.dialogue:9`

```diff
- Wyneth Hallow-Tide: Ask me again once you've earned the rest of it.
+ Wyneth Hallow-Tide: Ask me again if you want the rest of it. I won't dress it up.
```

## Old Grumbrand — The Last Reading

Quest description location: `quests/old_grumbrand_the_last_reading.tres:9`

```diff
- Old Grumbrand has gone quiet at the hearth, and a Kaan going quiet is never nothing. Earn enough of his trust in the field and he'll finally say why.
+ Old Grumbrand has gone quiet at the hearth, and a Kaan going quiet is never nothing. Ask what's starting.
```

Dialogue follow-up location: `dialogue/companions/old_grumbrand.dialogue:9`

```diff
- Old Grumbrand: Ask me again once you've earned the rest of it. I'm not telling that to just anyone who notices.
+ Old Grumbrand: Ask me again if you want the plain answer. I'm not offering it twice.
```
