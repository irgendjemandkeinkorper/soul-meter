# Roadmap — from a complete Chapter 1 to a playable, polished one

Status: DRAFT 2026-10-30, revised 2026-10-31 (second pass: slice beat, Hollow, 20 h/week calendar) for owner review

**Vision.** Chapter 1 still earns its first public hour the way Fallout did: the player reaches
the field early and finds credible ways through objects, people, barter, loot and travel because
the Phase 1 authoring makes DRAMGID and ruling 7 answer the build. Its first **proven** identity
beat comes after that foundation and the Phase 2 Soul pass: an ambient `dorthkor-vanguard` fight
on Dorthkor Road where alerted mobs stream into one readable CT order, Old Grumbrand exposes an
enemy's intent through Sacred Clarity, and Serai-Lun records or acts on the killing/mercy line
without an ungrounded attack on Vex. Phase 3 therefore has to add a staged reinforcement chain,
visible alert and admission cues, pending-reinforcement CT treatment, readable intent and the
minimum durable Serai reaction that otherwise belonged to Phase 4; Phase 4 still widens that
proof into a speaking company. The cost remains explicit: the first hour is a freedom-and-build
foundation rather than an identity-complete slice, the proof waits through Phases 1 and 2, and a
hostile companion turn remains owner-gated until there is an approved cause and consequence.

Designer's reading of the shipped gameplay systems and loops against the ten rulings in
`docs/game-identity.md` and the PRD metrics in
`docs/prd-chapter-one.md` §3, followed by a phased plan. It re-derives sequence from the measured
state of the repository at `9e1ee0f9`; `docs/roadmap-chapter-one.md` (2026-08-07) and
`docs/fleet-roadmap.md` (2026-09-02) keep their scope statements, and where this document orders
work differently, this one is the proposal and theirs is the record.

Evidence: three code inventories taken 2026-10-30 (combat; gates, playtest and UX; field,
narrative and economy), the ship-readiness assessment of 2026-10-16
(`docs/status/2026-10-16-ship-readiness-assessment.md`), and the full test run of the same day
(2766/2766). Inventory claims were read in code, not played; items marked *unverified* were seen
in a grep and still need a runtime check. Numbers marked PROVISIONAL in code stay PROVISIONAL here.

## 1. Where the game is

The build is systems-complete and content-thin, and its one identity-level mechanic runs
backwards. Every layer the PRD asked for exists: DRAMGID creation, the 22 skills, three hubs and
five wilds with interiors, travel with encounter scheduling, same-map combat with positional
depth, casting with Breath and Soul overreach, ten class resources, forty perks, the Reputation
and Renown ledgers with 184 band-gated lines, four main quests and eleven authored side quests,
saves with migrations, 303 test suites with no skips. Gate T is at 8 of 10, with only the two
human-gated criteria open (tester sessions, rendered reference runs).

What a player would feel, in order of weight:

| Loop | Intent (ruling) | What the build does | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chapter (Soul) | Every act spends soul; the meter trends down; Agreement is the only income (1, 2, 3) | Income up to ~96 across the chapter; scripted spend is two Iris lines (−6, −8) plus optional overreach. A careful player ends at 100. Hollowing is unreachable in ordinary play | **Inverted** |
| Encounter | Frequent, deep, grid mastery its own reward (4, 6) | The player's grammar is rich; the enemy's is one verb: strike the first living ally. 6 archetypes, 12 encounters, spawn tables draw from 2 enemies. Two timing models (AP default, CT for ambient) | **One-sided** |
| Company | Party of 4–6; elegiac and wry (5, 10) | Party of 3. Each companion: two dialogue titles, one hollowing bark, one recovery bark. No banter, no reactions to bands or Soul | **Small and silent** |
| Hub / field | Fallout-full: checks, locks, stealing, barter, loot (7) | Verbs implemented; one lock, one pocket, a shop screen with a passive Sway ratio and no live barter check, `loom_penalty` has no caller on main (PR #529 wires it), two loot tables, 22 items | **Built, not authored** |
| Travel | Journeys with encounters, avoid prompts, spoils | Works. The region map calls `GameFlow.start_journey()` and the selection/arrival legs have integration coverage, but the complete path still needs one end-to-end capture; Zhavar ladder has no production writer, so its toll, telegraph and rumour routes are unreachable | **Half-lit** |
| Progression | XP levels, skill points, perks at 2/4/6 (9) | All sources pay; perks pick and show. No level-up notice (*unverified*) | **Sound, mute** |
| Hours | 8–12 h (PRD §3) | Estimated 2–4 h (2026-10-16) | **Short** |

The table ranks the loops by how far each sits from its ruling; the plan in §4 does not follow
that rank. It authors the field first (the owner's chosen first feeling, and the loop with the
lowest prose cost per hour of play), then fixes the meter against that denser world, then gives
the enemy a game, then widens the company, and only then pours hours in. The Vision paragraph
above states what that order costs.

## 2. The loops, read closely

### 2.1 The chapter loop: Soul

**Intent.** Ruling 1 makes Soul the hook: every meaningful act spends a piece of it and the world
keeps a ledger. Ruling 3 says it returns only through Agreement and trends down across a chapter.
Pillar 4 of the design document: "Power is temptation, and the meter is the bill." The vault's
`../dramgid-vault/cosmology/souls.md` ("The Soul Gauge" and "Reaching zero") agrees: magic
spends the Gauge, it mostly only goes down, zero leaves the player alive and husked, and any
recovery is slow, social and permanently incomplete. It does not set this roadmap's numeric
ceiling; R13 owns that PROVISIONAL value.

**Build.** The spend side, in full:

- Dialogue: `dialogue/iris_illepah.dialogue`, −6 and −8. Nothing else in 29 dialogue files.
- Combat actions with a `soul_cost`: Jam the Gears 1, Paradox Strike 3, Sequenced Verdict 1.
- Overreach: casting past Breath pays Soul at Note 1 / Phrase 2 / Song 4 / Refrain 8
  (`globals/combat/resolution.gd`, PROVISIONAL), plus a fizzle `soul_failure_cost`. Breath
  refills on every scene entry (`ui/flow/game_flow.gd` → `GameState.refill_party_breath()`), so
  overreach happens only when a player chooses it mid-fight.

The income side: +8 per act of Agreement on five Dom side quests, six companion quests and the
Hollowmere stake (`globals/quest_registry.gd`), up to ~96. Start is 50; the clamp is 100.

**Read-back.** Hollowing-gated dialogue is seven lines in three files (`hadrik_vale`,
`sella_varn`, `marshal_coiljaw`). Companion barks: one line per companion for hollowing, one for
recovery. `NpcReactions` routes on hollowing. The 40–60 EQUILIBRIUM window gates the Marshal's
ruling. Nothing reads the *trend*; nothing reads a band between 1 and 39 except hollowing itself.

**Diagnosis.** The economy was built as a reward system with a cost system still to come. Open
question Q2 in `docs/chapter-one-open-questions.md`, the Definition and Paradox trees that were to
be the spend side, was never ruled. The result is that rulings 1–3 are true in the documents and
false on the gauge. This is the highest-leverage fix in the project, and it is mostly data: the
side-quest outcome arrays already carry `outcome_soul_deltas` and `resolve_side_quest` already
writes Soul, so a Soul-spending route is one more authored outcome per quest, not a new system.

### 2.2 The encounter loop

**Intent.** Ruling 4: combat is the tactical centrepiece, frequent and deep, Soul one resource
among several. Ruling 6: same-map, alerted mobs join. PRD §3: ≥80% of encounters use three or
more verbs.

**Build, player side (strong).** Elevation (+4 hit, +10% damage per step), facing (×1.10 flank,
×1.25 rear), cover (+2 defence); Gate T-2 passes (positional harness 2 vs naive 0). Defining
Strikes, called shots, injuries, Guard, Jam, Holds. Ten class resources. Forty perks in play with
34 effect ops. Casting with elements, Breath, overreach and fizzle. Sacred Clarity scans and locks
enemy intents; Read Aloud shares forecasts.

**Build, enemy side (weak).** `CombatController.plan_enemy_turn` picks one action, strike, aimed
by expected value, at the first living ally; it moves only when nothing is in reach. No retreat,
no focus fire, no abilities, no reinforcements. `Battle._resolve_enemy_round` is dead code. Six
archetypes; twelve encounters, five of them canon; five spawn tables that draw only on bog-wight
and loam-maddened boar. Counting attack and cast as given, eight of twelve encounters author a
third verb, seven of them only through the zone layouts of test and trial fights; two author
speech or context, and one of the five canon encounters passes
(`tools/audit_encounter_verbs.gd`). Five balance bands, three with effects. Enemy stat variation
(#412) awaits a ruling. On the populated-field 100-hostile benchmark, uncapped headless AI
decision time is PROVISIONAL 7.05–7.11 ms mean / PROVISIONAL 8.74–8.81 ms p95 as of 2026-10-15
(`docs/performance-benchmark.md:628-671`); the D9 2 ms per-decision line is advisory, while the
ratified FR-904 frame-time gate remains controlling.

**Scheduler.** AP is the shipped default and is labelled a shim; ambient fights force CT;
authored encounters lack the flag and run AP. The CT timeline HUD region, ruling 6's wording
("join the CT order"), and the slice-5 intent design ("this round") all assume CT.

**Diagnosis.** Depth is one-sided. The player has twenty answers and the enemy asks one question.
Tactics collapse to "kill whichever one reaches you first", and the positional system has nobody
to outmanoeuvre. The intent-and-lock machinery from slice 5 is the right skeleton for the fix:
give each archetype a small set of intents, make the planner choose among them with a threat
score, and Sacred Clarity's reveal becomes a tactical read instead of a formality.

### 2.3 The company loop

**Intent.** Ruling 5: 4–6 on the map. Ruling 10: elegiac and wry, gallows humour from
companions. Agreement rulings (2026-10-29): companion quests resolved well pay +8.

**Build.** Tavern recruitment chooses exactly two; the party is Vex + 2. Six companions, two
dialogue titles each. `CompanionBarks` has one hollowing line and one recovery line per
companion. No reaction to faction bands, Soul level, hub arrival, quest outcomes or each other.
"Banter" does not appear in the repository. Companion quests are `FlagQuest`s; the consequence
audit counts zero outcomes for them.

**Diagnosis.** The tone ruling is carried almost entirely by the companions, and they are nearly
silent. The Soul read-back ruling 2 names "companion reactions" as its first consequence and the
build has twelve lines. A party of three on a 100-mob map is also under the ruling's floor. This
is content more than code, but it needs two small systems: bark triggers on band and Soul
transitions, and a banter beat inside `TravelPlan`.

### 2.4 The hub and field loop

**Intent.** Ruling 7: skill checks on objects, lockpicking, stealing, barter, loot; stats matter
outside combat. Owner ruling 2026-09-08: Loom-sensitive skills degrade in Hush/Waning zones via
`SkillCheck.loom_penalty` reading `AccordZone`.

**Build.** Dialogue is well-gated: 184 Reputation band conditions and 16 `check(...)` gates.
The world is not: one authored lock (`world/dorthkor_road.tscn`, strain), one pickpocket (Toma
Reedhand), two field loot tables, 22 items, 14 vendors. Barter is a passive ratio from Sway:
`ui/screens/shop.gd` shows prices but makes no live check, and `GameState.barter_ratio()` never
moves in view. `loom_penalty` is implemented in
`globals/skill_check.gd` and has no production caller on the audited branch; PR #529 wires it for
interactable, chest and pickpocket checks. Equipment slots are saved in `globals/party_member.gd`;
the audit verified that no combat file reads them. Fast travel works in `ui/flow/game_flow.gd` and
`globals/fast_travel_registry.gd`; `ui/screens/region_map.gd` calls `GameFlow.start_journey()`,
`test/integration/test_region_map.gd` covers plan creation, and
`test/integration/test_travel_flow.gd` covers arrival. The remaining Phase 1 work is an end-to-end
capture and correction of any demonstrated gap. (The wilds scene keeps its legacy filename
`world/test_room.tscn`; it is the authored Loamroot Grove map, not a placeholder.)

**Diagnosis.** Stats matter in conversation and barely in the world. Ten of 22 skills are checked
anywhere in Chapter 1 content (owner item 11). Every verb exists once as a proof and needs an
authoring pass with a density target, which is itself an open identity question (loot density).

### 2.5 The travel and world loop

**Build.** `GameFlow.start_journey` builds a `TravelPlan` with an `EncounterDirector` schedule
and an avoid prompt; the WorldClock advances four phases and a day counter; spoils tables pay
journey wins; `SpawnDirector` repopulates by location policy. Weather exists per battle only. No
day/night visual (*unverified*). The Zhavar ladder's five rungs are saved; `raise_zhavar` is
raised in production for the `wilds` zone only (`globals/quest_registry.gd:542`,
`dialogue/sella_varn.dialogue:12`), so the toll, the telegraph and the Zhavar rumour routes on
the other zones are authored content nobody can reach.

**Diagnosis.** The travel loop is the game's natural pacing device and it currently carries
encounters only. A banter beat, a weather roll that persists into the arrival scene, and a live
Zhavar ladder would make each journey a story beat for very little code.

### 2.6 The progression loop

**Build.** Kill XP scaled by level gap, quest XP 40, milestone XP, cap 6, three skill points per
level (PROVISIONAL), perks at 2/4/6, 22-point attribute buy capped at 5. Nothing in the UI
connects to `xp_awarded` (*unverified*).

**Diagnosis.** Sound skeleton, no dopamine. A level-up notice and a perk-pick prompt are the
cheapest wins in this document.

### 2.7 The ledgers and what reads them

Reputation: append-only, bands at −40/−15/+15/+40, ~12 factions in content, 184 readers.
Renown: five Fame tiers and seven Karma tiers; readers are `NpcReactions` (5 band titles, 7 fame
titles, Upright/Troubled karma), perk gates, tavern recruit locks and the Sway modifier. No
dialogue condition calls `Renown.fame_tier_at_least`; fame-gated offers from the Yothmeru packet
are not built. Karma has writers now (quests, theft capped at 25 per scene, pickpocketing, six
dialogue grants). The chapter-complete screen reports two factions and counts "RESOLVED n / 10"
over Dom side quests only, so the Hollowmere stake does not show.

## 3. Findings, ranked by leverage

1. **The Soul economy is inverted.** Rulings 1–3 are the game's identity and the gauge cannot
   show them. Fix is mostly authored data plus read-back lines. Needs rulings R1–R3, R12 and R13.
2. **The enemy does not play the game.** One verb, one target rule, six archetypes, two in spawn
   tables. Positional depth has no opponent. Needs rulings R5, R10 and R15 (R4 is fixed by ruling 6).
3. **Two clocks.** AP shim by default, CT for ambient fights, a HUD and a ruling written for CT.
   Players cannot build timing intuition across two models. Needs no ruling: ruling 6 already
   names the CT order, so retiring the AP shim is Phase 3 work.
4. **The company is small and silent.** Party of 3 against a 4–6 ruling; twelve companion lines
   carry the tone ruling. Needs ruling R6 and prose.
5. **Hours.** 2–4 h estimated against an 8–12 h floor. Content-bound; sequence it last so it is
   written against the fixed economy and the finished enemy grammar. Governed by fixed R8 =
   `hold`.

Leverage is not sequence. §4 authors the field (the first "smaller" item below) before findings
1–4 because it is the owner's chosen first feeling and the cheapest per owner-hour; the Vision
paragraph carries the argument.

Also, smaller: field verbs authored once each; `loom_penalty` unwired; fame never read in
dialogue; Zhavar ladder unreachable; no level-up notice; chapter-complete count excludes
Hollowmere; the `loom_penalty` note in `docs/game-identity.md` is stale; Spanish is 132 of 134
msgids untranslated.

### The slice

The first proven beat is **C+reaction**, not C+D: take the combat route through the ambient
`dorthkor-vanguard` encounter on Dorthkor Road with Vex, ungated Serai-Lun and Old Grumbrand.
Alerts bring successive hostiles into the live CT order; Grumbrand, the only Stuid companion, makes
Sacred Clarity's intent reveal part of the read; Serai gives a killing/mercy refusal or departure,
persisted as the reaction outcome. She does not turn hostile. No companion has an established cause to
attack Vex, so a hostile turn needs an owner-approved cause, trigger and post-fight disposition
before it can enter a later plan (`globals/game_state.gd`, `globals/combat/combat_controller.gd`).

The mechanical spine already exists: alert and chain radii in `actors/hostile/hostile.gd`,
one-hop-per-measure propagation in `world/field_map.gd`, admission without a battle restart in
`globals/battle.gd`, and the current CT strip in
`ui/hud/regions/ct_timeline/ct_timeline_region.gd`. What does not exist is the demonstration:
staggered authored placements, a visible pre-alert telegraph, an admission event, a
colour-independent pending-reinforcement row and side marker, a reliable intent reveal for the
admitted enemy, and save-compatible Serai trigger, bark, persistence and outcome data. Deployed
set-pieces currently use AP and never drive the chain, so the Phase 3 proof remains this ambient
fight even after authored set-pieces exist.

Phase 3 owns the rendered proof after the fixed Phase 1 field and Phase 2 Soul order. It borrows
only the minimum Serai bark/trigger/persistence/outcome bundle from Phase 4; the general bark
matrix, banter, companion-quest outcomes and main-ruling interjections stay in Phase 4.
`dorthkor-vanguard` may remain avoidable in the shipped chapter, but its combat route is
mandatory for slice acceptance evidence.

### 5.1 R10, argued

Ruling 5 ratifies a capacity, "up to ~100 hostile mobs on a map at once"; ruling 6 says alerted
off-screen mobs join the CT order. Today admission is binary: `Hostile.alert_radius` (PROVISIONAL
320 px) and `FieldMap.propagate_alerts` chain-admit every hostile in range, and
`globals/combat/combat_controller.gd` has no cap constant, so every alerted hostile is a decider
(PROVISIONAL 94–95 reached in the chain-alert benchmark).

- **For a ceiling now (the designer's opening position).** An intent planner multiplies
  per-decider cost, and ~100 simultaneous deciders is more than facing, elevation, cover and
  Sacred Clarity reveals can make legible; a PROVISIONAL 24–32 ceiling bounds both before
  encounter content depends on uncapped behaviour.
- **Against (the position this roadmap adopts).** The uncapped headless baseline is PROVISIONAL
  7.05–7.11 ms mean after the 2026-10-15 fixes, so there is no measured performance need; a
  ceiling delays alerted joins, which is a change to ruling 6, and it costs present/engaged
  state, a queue, a cheap simulation for the unengaged and a HUD rule; tiered planning (every
  alerted hostile stays in CT, distant ones take cheap intents) can bound CPU without touching
  ruling 6; and the 24–32 number has no usability evidence behind it.

  `docs/owner-decisions.md`, and the roadmap is not authorization for commits or GitHub writes.
