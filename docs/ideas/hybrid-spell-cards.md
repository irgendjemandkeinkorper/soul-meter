# Hybrid spell cards: complete listed-family draft

**2026-09-09 · Initial playtest data; no runtime changes.** Uses the
[shared card rules](spell-card-rules.md), [elemental cards](elemental-spell-cards.md)
and [Khash material rules](khash-prototype-spell-cards.md). All 40 natural-hybrid forms
and the 12 listed advanced strained forms receive a card here or a precise pointer.
This completes the existing named catalog, not every possible distant-element pair.

| Form | N / Note | P / Phrase | S / Song | R / Refrain |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AP / CT | 2 / 30 | 3 / 45 | 4 / 60 | 4 / 60 |
| Breath | 3 | 6 | 12 | 24 |

Both components and the authored technique must be learned. Natural Chords select
one full hostile imposition; the table states that selection where an attack uses
one. Support intent never applies an unwanted hostile status to its recipient.
Both rule bends remain available only through the explicitly authored effects.
One cast pays once and rolls fizzle once; affected creatures use separate normal
hit results. Resolve each entity/source/effect once across an area or route.

## Weakened hostile statuses for the first prototype

The catalog defines weakened impositions for Strained Chords but does not supply a
universal numeric scaling here. **Proposed card convention:** halve a timed hostile
status's checkpoint count, round up, minimum one. Keep its per-event value unchanged.
Thus Burning/Soaked/Blinded/Shocked last one checkpoint and Decaying lasts two.
One-use restrictions still expire at that shortened deadline. Instant Tempo reset
remains instant; direct attack power is already explicitly budgeted and is not
silently halved a second time. This is new tuning to validate, not a claim about the
current runtime. Support extensions, holds and physical Wet are separately authored
effects and do not inherit hostile-imposition scaling automatically.

An ongoing field uses its own authored hazard/status contract. The inherited
Firebreak field still applies its stated two-checkpoint Burning; it does not add
Vekh's Blinded or repeat a cast's initial impositions at every hazard tick. The
weakened-duration rule applies to the explicitly declared direct hostile application,
such as Held Ember's pulse, rather than silently rewriting every downstream effect.

## Validation targets

Every listed family must preserve its pair through its forms, one cast payment,
explicit selected/weakened impositions, unique target/effect/source accounting and
the shared Refrain allowance. No hybrid can turn a missing source or full recipient
into a refund loop. No concealment hides physical consequences, no quench repairs
integrity, and no moving field resets an already-used damage event.
