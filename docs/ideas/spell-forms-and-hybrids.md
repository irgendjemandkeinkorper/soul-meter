# Spell forms, ultimates and hybrids

**2026-09-09 · Working design proposal.** Extends the
[elemental magic systems draft](elemental-magic-systems.md). Spell names, new effects,
unlock requirements and ultimate restrictions below are candidates for playtesting.
This document does not change runtime spells, costs, classes or the generated Wheel.

The [complete spell-card packet](spell-card-catalog.md) now prices every named form
and defines its target, duration and counter. Explicit card refinements supersede
this earlier concept pass, including the Khor ultimate prices and Unfading Brand's
limited field reposition. The concept names, Wheel pairs and elemental identities remain.
The [strained-family supplement](strained-spell-cards.md) subsequently adds 78 forms
for the remaining 26 legal distant pairs. The 92-name inventory below records this
original concept pass; the packet index tracks the expanded 170-form total.

## Two independent choices: form and composition

| Player-facing form | Proposed magnitude | Tactical purpose | Why keep earlier forms? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Note | A precise, immediate application of an element. | Low commitment and narrow targeting remain useful when resources or space are tight. |
| Secondary | Phrase | Add a deliberate choice: a second target, a direction, a conversion or a response. | Primary avoids paying for a capability the situation does not need. |
| Tertiary | Song | Establish a field, a route or a continuing setup that changes positioning. | Earlier forms act without maintaining a field or preparing a route. |
| Ultimate | Refrain | Create a brief decisive opportunity with a visible commitment and counter. | An ultimate has a limited use and can affect friendly preparations or physical surroundings. |

This is the proposed authoring convention for these families, not a change to the
four existing magnitude ids. Forms are learned options; they do not replace each
other and do not require casting the earlier forms in sequence unless a working
explicitly uses their setup. A spell's magnitude does not multiply every property.

**Breadth remains separate:** a single-element Tone can have an ultimate form; a
two-element Chord can have a primary form. Triads obey their existing casting gates
and have their own cataloged identity. Tertiary does not mean three elements, and
ultimate does not mean every element at once.

## Proposed commitment rules

1. Primary and secondary workings use their authored normal action/Breath costs.
   Tertiary workings disclose placement and any upkeep before commitment. Existing
   fizzle, overreach, hit and defense rules still apply where relevant.
2. **Initial combat prototype: one committed Refrain per character per encounter.**
   This is a usage limit, not a new energy meter. A rejected action consumes no use;
   a committed fizzle consumes the use and follows existing failure costs. This
   severe fizzle tradeoff is specifically a playtest question, not ratified tuning.
3. Refrains need the family's learned mastery and the normal casting resources.
   Universal Khor access grants its basic technique, not its ultimate. A banked
   Vicoar token can secure a cast through the existing hook; it does not add an
   ultimate use. Kero's guaranteed hit does not remove fizzle.
4. Every Refrain shows its target area or commitment before resolution, has a
   defined response opportunity if it has a wind-up, and states what survives.
   Exact wind-up/action costs remain authored per spell; instant casts do not
   pretend to offer an interrupt window. No ultimate grants Soul.
5. Khor/Vel cannot refresh an ultimate-use limit or recursively extend the
   ultimate's own special window. Out-of-combat Refrain access needs a separate
   authored recovery rule before shipping; opening and closing combat cannot
   become a resource-harvesting method.

## Elemental spell families

Each table develops one representative family through four forms. A mature element
can have additional attack, support and exploration families; these are not claims
that every spell in an element must follow this exact sequence. Support forms must
explicitly opt out of direct damage, even when their element can also author attacks.

### Sul — revelation

| Form | Working | Effect and meaningful limit |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | **Unveil** | Reveal eligible concealed magical properties on one visible target. Does not reveal rooms through opaque walls. |
| Secondary | **Prism Lance** | Attack along an authored line and expose an eligible signature on the struck target. Alignment matters; revelation does not ignore physical cover. |
| Tertiary | **Witness Light** | Establish a bounded revelation field where allies plan to fight. Information remains current only while targets meet its visibility rules. |
| Ultimate | **Noonday Revelation** | Sweep the declared area for eligible illusions and concealed workings, then keep that area revealed for the authored window. Creates an informed attack opportunity; does not invent weaknesses or automatically cancel what it reveals. |

### Vel — growth

| Form | Working | Effect and meaningful limit |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | **Briar Touch** | Apply the authored Overgrown imposition to one creature. The status alone does not grow a terrain obstacle. |
| Secondary | **Cultivate** | Extend one of the caster's own eligible buffs up to its extension ceiling. Recasting cannot accumulate infinite duration. |
| Tertiary | **Rootwork** | Grow a declared obstruction from eligible living substrate. Legal footprint, occupied cells and growth lifetime are explicit. |
| Ultimate | **Crown of Seasons** | Establish a grove with authored clear routes and growth obstacles; apply one bounded extension to the caster's own eligible buffs within its rules. The grove shapes a position; it does not repeatedly refresh every ally's buffs. |

### Luth — water and Breath

| Form | Working | Effect and meaningful limit |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | **Douse** | Soak a creature or wet a separately eligible surface. Creature and surface states are distinct. |
| Secondary | **Second Breath** | Restore an ally's Breath within the draft's paid-Breath budget. This support form does not damage the recipient. |
| Tertiary | **Turning Tide** | Choose a support distribution among eligible allies or quenching across a declared area. Intent and budget are fixed at commitment; it does not secretly grant both full effects. |
| Ultimate | **Great Confluence** | Distribute one fixed restoration budget among declared allies while quenching eligible fire in the declared refuge. The total restoration stays within Breath actually paid; wider reach does not create a net-positive casting loop or restore structural integrity. |

### Tham — weight and fortification

| Form | Working | Effect and meaningful limit |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | **Ballast** | Apply the authored Weighted imposition to a creature. It does not automatically stun or prevent every movement action. |
| Secondary | **Anchor** | Anchor an eligible Aftertone, protecting it from the existing unanchored-consumption channel. This does not freeze its lifetime. |
| Tertiary | **Raise Cover** | Place temporary cover in a valid, unoccupied footprint. Navigation, line of sight and its visible shape must agree. |
| Ultimate | **Citadel** | Place a declared formation of temporary cover with visible access gaps and shelter positions. Reject illegal footprints before payment; no entombing actors, restoring ruined buildings or leaving invisible collision after expiry. |

### Vekh — concealment

| Form | Working | Effect and meaningful limit |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | **Veil** | Conceal an eligible casting signature. The caster's body and physical actions are not automatically invisible. |
| Secondary | **Blindside** | Conceal the preparation of a selected working until its authored reveal point. Its manifestation still supplies the tells needed to respond. |
| Tertiary | **Shroud** | Establish a bounded area that conceals eligible friendly magical signatures. Concealment follows visibility rules and does not remove collision or ordinary sound. |
| Ultimate | **Eclipse Procession** | Carry a bounded concealment field with the caster, supporting a coordinated reposition and spell sequence. Allies must remain inside; ordinary revelation can contest eligible signatures. This does not inherit Barrow's exceptional prohibition on enemy reveal effects. |

### Mozh — decay and reclamation

| Form | Working | Effect and meaningful limit |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | **Wither** | Apply the authored Decaying imposition to a living target. Decay is not an instant execution or automatic resource harvest. |
| Secondary | **Reclaim** | Consume one eligible corpse or expendable object for a finite Breath return. Preview the permanent loss and exclude protected quest objects. |
| Tertiary | **Rot the Brace** | Degrade a susceptible structural component and disclose the path/support consequence. Eligibility is material-specific; all stone does not become rotten wood. |
| Ultimate | **Last Harvest** | Apply decay in a declared area and reclaim a finite selected set of eligible remains there. Living targets must survive the normal damage rules; no automatic harvest of creatures merely because they are inside the area. |

Created spell objects and repeated save/load cannot become new harvest sources by
default. Stable source identity and an authored yield must survive consumption.

### Khash — ignition and expenditure

| Form | Working | Effect and meaningful limit |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | **Kindle** | Apply Burning through an authored attack or ignite an eligible physical material. Target mode determines which state changes. |
| Secondary | **Cinder Spear** | Commit a directed fire strike and consume an eligible unanchored Aftertone for the existing burst benefit. The player can see which preparation is being spent. |
| Tertiary | **Firebreak** | Establish a bounded magical fire line that contests a route. Separately ignited combustible objects can keep burning after the line ends. |
| Ultimate | **Crown of Embers** | Resolve a declared pattern of fire bursts using a bounded selection of eligible unanchored Aftertones. Each selected trace is spent once; physical ignition follows material rules. It does not gain Cinderfall's exceptional consumption of all Aftertones on both sides. |

### Zhem — subtraction

| Form | Working | Effect and meaningful limit |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | **Quiet** | Zero one target's Tempo through the established rule. No direct elemental damage. |
| Secondary | **Sever** | Remove an eligible buff or Aftertone, including an eligible held Note. Choose the setup worth removing; this is not physical repair. |
| Tertiary | **Still Choir** | Resolve a bounded suppression sweep across declared targets and their eligible ongoing effects. It does not automatically prohibit all subsequent actions or enable global tile Hush. |
| Ultimate | **Final Cadence** | End eligible ongoing magic and reset Tempo across a declared area, including affected friendly preparations. It can dismiss a temporary construct whose life depends on that magic; ordinary fire, wounds and rubble remain. No damage, instant death or battle reset. |

### Zhur — lightning and routes

| Form | Working | Effect and meaningful limit |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | **Arc** | Make an authored lightning attack with the established Instability-die exception. It does not remove all fizzle or hit uncertainty. |
| Secondary | **Forked Arc** | Divide the working along two declared eligible targets under one authored budget. It does not duplicate a full single-target attack for free. |
| Tertiary | **Conduct** | Route a working through a connected eligible conductive surface. Preview the route, reach limit and affected friendlies before commitment. |
| Ultimate | **Storm Circuit** | Resolve a declared connected network of lightning paths, with a finite target budget and no repeated hit from circuit loops. Positioning the network is the setup; tile charge alone does not prove physical conductivity. |

### Khor — shared, with earned mastery

| Form | Working | Effect and meaningful limit |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | **Hold Note** | Deliberately sustain one owned, eligible Note through its next upkeep checkpoint under the shared-Khor proposal. |
| Secondary | **Carry the Note** | Move a specifically mobile held effect with its caster. Anchored and location-bound effects remain fixed. |
| Tertiary | **Passing Tone** | Hand the hold to a willing ally with an available sustain slot. Remaining duration and ownership stay unchanged; the receiver accepts the normal upkeep. There is never a duplicate effect. |
| Ultimate | **Unbroken Refrain** | Prepay one round of upkeep for one held effect, allowing its holder to act during that window without spending the maintenance action. It remains removable and ends normally if its life conditions fail. Does not sustain itself or grant extra sustain slots. |

Khor's separate Hold Note technique can support an eligible existing working without
turning its original composition into a Khor hybrid. A Khor-containing Chord still
needs a legal pair and the proper gate. No Khor form deals direct damage.

## Hybrid access and legality

Learn both components and the authored hybrid technique; choosing a major element
does not grant all secondary elements. Shared Khor makes Khor available as a
component, while its mastery and breadth gates remain. Patron selection affects
the existing resource hooks, not elemental legality.

| Hybrid kind | Existing rule preserved | Design consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Adjacent Chord | One selected full imposition; both elements supply their rule bends. | Declare the dominant imposition per working. Do not silently apply two full statuses. |
| Strained Chord | Two-to-four-step separation; weakened impositions and the existing strain/Vär and fizzle rules. | Additional flexibility carries the established cost and risk; it is not a discounted adjacent Chord. |
| Opposed pair | Five steps apart on the retained Wheel; disallowed in one composition. | Successive casts can interact with resulting world states. They do not constitute a legal hybrid spell. |
| Triad | One of the ten cataloged adjacent triples, with its defined center and unique effect. | An ultimate can express that identity, but cannot swap the center or inherit every wing's full damage. |

## Natural Chord families

All ten adjacent pairs are represented below. These named techniques are proposals
for explicit authored effects, not automatic consequences already granted by the
generic composition resolver. Higher forms retain the same legal two-element pair.

| Components | Primary · Note | Secondary · Phrase | Tertiary · Song | Ultimate · Refrain |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sul + Vel** | **Dawnseed:** reveal an eligible trace around an existing growth effect. | **Prism Briar:** extend an owned growth buff while revealing a declared nearby signature. | **Witness Grove:** establish a growth position with a bounded revelation area. | **Garden of First Light:** reveal eligible magic across the grove and apply one capped extension to the caster's own eligible growth buffs; no copying every ally's buffs. |
| **Vel + Luth** | **Dewbud:** wet an eligible target and preserve an owned growth buff within its ceiling. | **Nourishing Flow:** direct a paid Breath restoration to an ally within the caster's growth setup. | **Living Spring:** establish a support position with a fixed restoration budget and eligible vegetation. | **Rain of Renewal:** distribute that finite paid budget across the declared refuge and extend eligible owned growth buffs once; no regenerating source or resurrection. |
| **Luth + Khor** | **Breathsong:** replenish a declared ally within the paid-Breath budget while maintaining an eligible owned support Note. | **Reservoir Hymn:** allocate that restoration into disclosed installments; the total budget is paid once and cannot grow. | **River Chorus:** sustain the installment schedule across eligible recipients under normal upkeep. | **Returning Tide:** redirect only the remaining prepaid installments among declared eligible allies; no fresh budget, repeated payout or unused-budget Soul refund. |
| **Khor + Tham** | **Keystone Note:** anchor and deliberately hold one eligible owned Aftertone. | **Resonant Brace:** extend the anchored support to a declared ally within its footprint, sharing one effect budget. | **Standing Chord:** maintain an authored support field around that anchor, consuming one sustain slot. | **Unbroken Bastion:** prepay a bounded hold window for the anchored field; its protection comes from that authored field, not universal damage immunity or Founding's freeze of every effect. |
| **Tham + Vekh** | **Veiled Anchor:** anchor an eligible Aftertone while concealing its magical signature. | **Blind Corner:** place a temporary cover segment that obscures eligible signatures behind its real footprint. | **Hidden Redoubt:** establish a bounded cover position and signature shroud. | **Citadel of Dusk:** deploy the declared cover/shroud formation with clear access gaps; physical walls remain visible and ordinary revelation can contest its concealed signatures. |
| **Vekh + Mozh** | **Veiled Wither:** conceal a decay working's preparation while leaving its manifestation readable. | **Quiet Reclaim:** consume an eligible finite source with a concealed casting signature. | **Hollow Veil:** maintain signature concealment around an explicitly bounded reclamation position. | **Nameless Harvest:** reclaim the declared finite remains within a shrouded area; no erased corpse ownership, hidden infinite sources or automatic execution of living targets. |
| **Mozh + Khash** | **Rotbrand:** apply the declared dominant imposition and spend an eligible unanchored Aftertone for fire burst power. | **Ash Reclaim:** burn an eligible expendable source and convert its authored recoverable yield once. | **Cinder Garden:** establish a bounded burn/decay position with a declared finite reclamation set. | **Funeral Furnace:** resolve that selected fuel and reclaim set once; source destruction cannot produce a second harvestable copy. Does not inherit Pyre's expired-Aftertone conversion. |
| **Khash + Zhem** | **Cauterizing Note:** make a fire-dominant strike and remove one declared eligible effect on that target. | **Ashen Severance:** declare separate eligible traces for fire consumption and removal; one trace cannot pay for both. | **Fire's End:** establish an authored suppression boundary and bounded fire attack pattern; resulting physical fires still need quenching. | **Last Ember:** resolve a declared fire-dominant attack and eligible magical removals across its area; if authored with a utility-only damage result, all direct fire damage remains disabled. |
| **Zhem + Zhur** | **Static Quiet:** attack with the declared lightning-dominant working and zero the eligible target's Tempo. | **Severing Arc:** remove a declared eligible effect alongside a lightning attack. | **Breaker's Circuit:** route a capped attack through declared eligible targets while stripping the specified effects. | **Storm of Silence:** resolve that bounded circuit and removals once per target; does not freeze Balance, guarantee every outcome or inherit Stillpoint's unique effect. |
| **Zhur + Sul** | **Spark of Witness:** strike a target and reveal an eligible concealed signature there. | **Prism Fork:** divide a declared lightning budget between aligned targets and expose eligible signatures. | **Beacon Circuit:** establish a finite conductive attack route with revelation at its declared nodes. | **Sky of Witnesses:** resolve the prepared circuit and a bounded revelation window; no chain outside the preview or free extra allied turn from Thunderhead. |

For every damage-bearing hybrid, the authored attack budget is shared across its
targets and paths. A Khor or Zhem rule bend is never an independent damage bonus.
For every support hybrid, suppress direct damage explicitly rather than inheriting
it just because one component is damage-capable. Every ultimate retains the same
material, restoration, consumption and sustain limits as its lower forms.

Implementation prerequisite: the chosen dominant imposition and attack/support
intent must travel through the actual forecast/commit path. The composition helper's
selection support alone does not establish that these authored options are wired
through a playable spell.

| **Vel + Tham · 3** | **Rootbrace:** reinforce an authored growth position with an anchored support effect. | **Living Rampart:** establish temporary cover interleaved with eligible vegetation and explicit gaps. | **Verdant Fortress:** place the validated formation and extend an eligible owned growth buff once. | Plant and stone components keep separate material properties; roots burning does not automatically ignite stone. |

## Opposed elements: sequences, not hybrid spells

| Disallowed same-cast pair | Legal encounter interaction to author |
|---|---|
| Sul + Vekh | Conceal a signature with Vekh, then contest that concealment with a separate Sul revelation. |
| Vel + Mozh | Grow an eligible physical obstruction, then decay it through a later, material-eligible working. |
| Luth + Khash | Ignite physical fuel, then quench it; a steam consequence would need its own authored reaction and is not assumed. |
| Khor + Zhem | Hold an eligible Note, then sever it with a separate working. |
| Tham + Zhur | Stone cover changes attack paths; whether a particular stone object conducts is a material rule, not automatic elemental opposition damage. |

## Triads: preserve the existing exceptional identities

These are the catalog's existing names and effect anchors, not newly invented
three-element families. They are not all newly implemented by this design. A
Refrain expression must preserve the named unique effect; its extra scale requires
authoring rather than treating the table as permission to increase every parameter.

| Triad | Components → center | Existing unique-effect anchor |
|---|---|---|
| **Dayspring** | Zhur + Sul + Vel → Sul | **First Light:** a revelation window covering the cataloged magical information and discovered weaknesses. |
| **Fruiting** | Sul + Vel + Luth → Vel | **Second Season:** copy active friendly buffs to the cataloged additional targets. This is a Triad exception to ordinary Vel's own-buff extension. |
| **Rivermouth** | Vel + Luth + Khor → Luth | **The Mouth Opens:** the cataloged window for area effects crossing zone boundaries and free zone changes; grid adaptation needs explicit scope. |
| **Founding** | Luth + Khor + Tham → Khor | **Cornerstone:** freeze remaining durations through its cataloged checkpoint. The utility center has no direct damage. |
| **Vault** | Khor + Tham + Vekh → Tham | **Sealed Ground:** fortification, protected anchored Aftertones and the cataloged zone-entry/ranged-attack effects. |
| **Barrow** | Tham + Vekh + Mozh → Vekh | **Unlisted:** the cataloged concealment and targeting/revelation restrictions. Ordinary Vekh spells do not inherit these exceptions. |
| **Pyre** | Vekh + Mozh + Khash → Mozh | **The Rendering:** the cataloged conversion of corpses, destroyed objects and expired Aftertones, with side-wide Breath distribution. Source eligibility and one-time consumption still require explicit implementation. |
| **Cinderfall** | Mozh + Khash + Zhem → Khash | **Everything Burns At Once:** the catalog's consumption of all Aftertones on both sides for the caster side's bursts. Its friendly-setup cost must be visible. |
| **Stillpoint** | Khash + Zhem + Zhur → Zhem | **The Held Silence:** the cataloged centering/lock and threshold suppression of Balance. It does not grant Soul or direct elemental damage. |
| **Thunderhead** | Zhem + Zhur + Sul → Zhur | **Nothing Is Uncertain:** the cataloged Instability exception and one out-of-turn ally opportunity. The name is not a blanket guarantee against every roll. |

## Art and combat readability

| Form or layer | Visual / audio direction | Required information |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | One clear element motif and brief attack or support gesture. | Target, hit location and whether this working attacks or helps. |
| Secondary | Add a visible fork, link or chosen direction to that motif. | The extra decision: recipient, line, conversion source or selected effect. |
| Tertiary | Use a stable ground boundary, route or anchor with a continuing sound. | Field ownership, upkeep/expiry and openings through any footprint. |
| Ultimate | Show the declared preparation, a recognizable release, and a restrained lingering state. | Response window, full affected area, spent setup and continuing physical hazards. |
| Hybrid | Show the dominant element's silhouette with the second element's identifiable motion or motif. | Components remain distinguishable; blended color alone cannot explain the effect. |

Use the existing approved elemental palettes and art direction. Khor's sustain
motif should read as a connection to its owned effect; it must not obscure the
effect's original identity. Sound cues supplement shape and labels. No full-screen
effect should hide an enemy response, a friendly target or a newly blocked route.

## First progression playtest

The [Khash prototype spell cards](khash-prototype-spell-cards.md) now propose concrete
AP/CT/Breath prices, ranges, field and fire rules, response windows, and save/travel
examples for this progression. Those values remain playtest candidates.

Develop **Kindle → Cinder Spear → Firebreak → Crown of Embers**, then add the
Khor/Khash strained family and Luth quenching as the first comparison. This tests
precision, spending an Aftertone, maintaining a route hazard, an ultimate commitment,
hybrid strain and persistent material consequences in the existing barricade yard.

Before implementing, author target ranges, action/Breath costs, hit/fizzle surfaces,
durations, upkeep, material damage and the Refrain's exact attack budget. Validate
that a primary remains useful, a utility cast never damages its recipient, a
consumed trace/source pays once, opposed pairs are refused without costs, and
quenching does not undo damage. These are required acceptance cases, not a claim
that a chart constitutes a finished spell implementation.
