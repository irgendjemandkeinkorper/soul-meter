# Elemental spell cards: complete family draft

**2026-09-09 · Initial playtest data; no runtime changes.** Read the
[shared card rules](spell-card-rules.md) for costs, statuses, target intent, checkpoint
timing, resource budgets and interruption. N/P/S/R specify both the form and its
default AP/CT/Breath price. Every exception appears on its card.

| Form | N — Note | P — Phrase | S — Song | R — Refrain |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AP / CT | 2 / 30 | 3 / 45 | 4 / 60 | 4 / 60 |
| Breath | 3 | 6 | 12 | 24 |

All pure-element families below use Tone breadth. Khash's four cards remain in
the [Khash prototype packet](khash-prototype-spell-cards.md). Together, the two
documents cover all nine chosen elements and the shared Khor family.

**Readability:** a decay marker with remaining ticks → the selected source/yield
before irreversible consumption → a visibly compromised brace → distinct living
targets and consumable remains in the ultimate preview.

| Form / working | Target / reach | Concrete effect | Counter / aftermath |
|---|---|---|---|
| N · **Quiet** | One creature within 4 | Reset Tempo once. No HP damage or continuing prohibition on casting. | A later legal action can generate Tempo normally. Do not invent a persistent lock from this reset. |
| P · **Sever** | One eligible buff, Aftertone or working anchor within 4 | Remove the selected effect instance, including an eligible held Note. Utility intent; no damage. | Does not remove arbitrary debuffs, physical combustion or rubble. The preview shows every footprint affected by removing a whole working. |
| S · **Still Choir** | Center within 4, radius 1 | Remove up to three selected eligible effect instances and reset Tempo on up to three declared creatures in the area. Silence should expose the aftermath rather than hide terrain in a full-screen flash.

## Khor — shared foundation and earned mastery

| Form / working | Target / reach | Concrete effect | Counter / aftermath |
|---|---|---|---|
| N · **Hold Note** | One owned eligible Note anchor within 4 | Exception: 1 AP / 30 CT, 1 Breath initially and each upkeep. Freeze remaining duration through the next checkpoint; one sustain slot. | Missing upkeep, leaving sustain reach or failing life conditions breaks the hold. No direct damage. |
| P · **Carry the Note** | One owned mobile-eligible held Note within 4 | Standard P price. Attach its mobile anchor to the holder's movement for two checkpoints, keeping the same instance/budget. Continue normal upkeep. | A location-bound or anchored effect rejects. Moving a field does not reset per-round hit accounting or double its effects. |
| S · **Passing Tone** | Current held Note plus a willing ally; both within 4, and ally within 4 of anchor | Standard S price. Atomically transfer upkeep to the ally's free slot; same original owner, duration and effect id. No new field or initial pulse. | Invalid recipient/slot rejects without releasing the original hold. Receiver must satisfy ongoing sustain reach and life conditions. |
| R · **Unbroken Refrain** | One already held eligible Note within 4; instant | **Exception: 0 AP / 0 CT, 9 Breath.** Prepay one checkpoint of upkeep, allowing the holder to spend that maintenance action elsewhere. No extra slot or new pulse. | Consumes the character's R use; still fizzles normally and remains removable. Cannot target its own special window. |

The Unbroken Refrain price is a completion-pass correction to the earlier generic
R price: paying 4 AP merely to save a later 1 AP made the intended action benefit
unusable. The proposed zero-action commitment still costs Breath, uses the shared
R allowance and follows normal failure rules. It is not an unlimited free action.

**Readability:** a tether to the actual owned effect → a mobile-anchor indicator
→ one tether visibly transferred between holders → a prepaid-upkeep marker. The
original elemental shape remains recognizable through every Khor overlay.

## Coverage and implementation boundary

These cards plus the Khash packet cover the 40 named pure/shared-Khor forms. Natural
and strained combinations have [their own card catalog](hybrid-spell-cards.md), and
existing [Triad compatibility cards](triad-spell-cards.md) preserve the catalog's
exceptions. Exact status behavior, target intent, moving fields, ownership transfer,
object reactions and R commitments are not implemented by these documents.
