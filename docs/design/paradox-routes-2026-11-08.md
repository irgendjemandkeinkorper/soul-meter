**Status:** PROPOSAL, 2026-11-08. Every line below is a draft for your edit. None is authored, and
nothing is built until you approve a route. All costs are PROVISIONAL (owner item 5).

**Rulings this follows:**

- **R1** (2026-10-31): one Soul-spending Paradox route on each of the ten Dom side quests and the
  Hollowmere stake, plus a Soul cost on each of the three main-quest rulings whichever way it goes.
  Costs are −4 to −10, shown before commitment.
- **SR3** (2026-11-08): every Paradox route is **always listed**, as a normal option with its
  SOUL −N chip. The cost is the only gate: no skill check, no tutorial lock.
- **R3:** start at 50; Paradox play ends at 30 or below; the all-Agreement path ends at 40–50.
- **Ruling 1** (`docs/game-identity.md`): every meaningful act spends a piece of your soul.
- **Ruling 3:** Soul returns only through acts of Agreement. A Paradox route never mints Soul.

## 1. What a Paradox route is

"Paradox" has no canon in the vault. It is the design term for Chapter 1's Soul-spend side (FR-104;
R1 answers Q2 of `docs/chapter-one-open-questions.md` "by routes", with the ability trees deferred).
In play, a Paradox route is:

> **A third way that refuses the either/or, paid out of your own Gauge.**

Each draft is one of two shapes, so the drafts invent no new magic:

1. **A working.** Canon: "magic spends the Gauge, and it mostly only goes down"
   (`../dramgid-vault/cosmology/souls.md`). The player does something by sound or song that the
   scene's two outcomes could not: settle ash, tune a seal, keep a beat. The effect stays local and
   literal.
2. **Spending yourself.** The player answers for someone else, under their own name. The cost is
   ruling 1's, not a new metaphysics.

A Paradox route is not an Agreement. Where a quest's Agreement outcome exists, the Paradox route
**forgoes its +8**, and it never re-Agrees a place. In the drafts, no route writes Reputation or
Karma (see question 3).

### The lines, per route

Each route needs five pieces of prose: the option, the consequence chip, the giver's reply, the
read-back question, and the read-back answer. The data row is for implementation; the outcome ids
are new.

**1. Dishonest Casks** — `ring-the-seals`, −6
- Option: "Let me tune the seals. If the water turns, the bronze will say so."
- Chip: *The forges keep sounding; a fouled cask rings before anyone drinks.*
- Keth Varr: "Bronze that tells on its own water. The Roar holds, and no hand drinks ash unwarned. I will not ask what it cost you; I can see some of it."
- Read-back: "Have the seals rung?" / "Twice since your working. Both casks were poured out before a cup was drawn, and the Hammer Roar never stopped."
- Cause: "Tuned the East Arm's cask seals to ring at foul water"

**2. Ash in the Rain** — `clear-the-cistern`, −8
- Option: "Don't choose. Let me sing the ash out of the whole cistern."
- Chip: *The whole reserve runs clean; both lines drink.*
- Vaara Cisternhand: "There is enough now. There was not enough an hour ago. Do not tell me how; I watched what it took out of you."
- Read-back: "Does the reserve answer both lines?" / "The hospice fire burns and the market line runs at dawn, both from a cistern that should not be this clean."
- Cause: "Sang the forge ash out of Vaara's cistern"

**3. Fifth Echo** — `carry-the-fifth`, −4
- Option: "Damp the drum. I'll carry the fifth beat and listen for it."
- Chip: *Dom keeps four clean beats; the warning lives in you.*
- Pell Hammersong: "Four beats for the city and five for you. You will flinch at every call now, a beat late. When the west speaks, I will ask you, not the drum."
- Read-back: "Does the brace answer cleanly now?" / "Four clean beats from the brace. You still turn west a beat after every call, and I still ask you what you heard."
- Cause: "Took the Jawbrace's fifth beat into your own hearing"
- Note: this does not say what the fifth beat is. The mystery stays open.

**4. Last Safe Course** — `pace-the-course`, −10
- Option: "Publish it. I'll keep time on the outer chain so the hulls go through one at a time."
- Chip: *Every pilot has the course; nobody drowns in a crowd. The beat is yours to keep.*
- Orren Chainwake: "Every lodge gets the marks and the chain moves to a beat. Yara says nobody on the wall is keeping it. I will not tell her it is you."
- Read-back: "What followed publication?" / "Fresh marks hang in every pilot lodge, and the hulls go through the sea-mouth one at a time, spaced by a beat on the chain that nobody claims."
- Cause: "Kept the outer chain's time so every hull could take the last safe course"

**5. Living Tag** — `carry-the-plate`, −6
- Option: "Give the guard back their name. I'll carry the plate to the Wound-Watch myself."
- Chip: *The name returns, the plate is evidence, and you are the only one who carried it.*
- Droma Flintjaw: "Both truths, then: the name on morning roll, the plate behind chain. You carried it the whole way and did not put it on. Good. You look like you carried more than iron."
- Read-back: "Does morning roll carry the guard again?" / "Morning roll speaks the guard's name, and the clean plate stands chained in the Wound-Watch. You still walk the long way round the screen."
- Cause: "Carried the living-tagged plate to the Wound-Watch yourself"
- Note: this implies the plate costs whoever carries it. That is new. Cut it if it reads as lore.

**6. Marching Knots** — `ring-the-knots`, −4
- Option: "The watch takes the net. I'll sing the knot order into the shrine bell for the harbor."
- Chip: *The evidence stays sealed and the harbor still hears the warning.*

- Read-back: "Do harbor hands heed the shrine bell?" / "The net lies under watch glass, and the river shrine rings the knot order at every change of watch. Dockhands stop talking until it finishes."

**7. Rainbound Register** — `both-columns`, −6
- Option: "Both columns. The name stays a witness and the road opens. Let me make the seals hold it."
- Chip: *The night road opens and the name stays WITNESS; the seals carry the contradiction.*

**8. Smoothed Weights** — `true-burden`, −6
- Option: "Don't void them all. Let me weigh what each candidate truly carried."
- Chip: *Only the altered ranks fall; no honest candidate repeats the Pillar.*

**9. Unclaimed Bed** — `my-name-on-the-debt`, −4

**11. Hollowmere stake** — `force-the-corner`, −10
- Option: "Don't nudge it. I'll make the corner hold, and you file the true reading anyway."
- Chip: *The corner holds by force and the map stays true. The place is not re-Agreed.*
- Mender Corrin Vell: "You did not nudge it. You told it. The corner holds, and I filed what the needle said before you finished. Verspch will want to know whose song that was."

## 4. Does this reach R3? (for owner item 5 and #599)

These figures use the roadmap's own: Agreement income is up to about **96**, which is 12 acts at +8.
`S` is the unavoidable spend that P2.6 (#599) sizes (set-piece overreach and the like). The rows show
where three kinds of player end the chapter.

| Path | Start | Income | Rulings | Paradox | End |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| All-Agreement, no Paradox | 50 | +96 | −20 | 0 | **126 − S**; 40–50 needs S ≈ 76–86 |
| Paradox only where no Agreement is lost (routes 1–4, 6, 8) | 50 | +96 | −20 | −38 | **88 − S**; about 2–12 at that S |
| Every Paradox route | 50 | +56 (5 Agreements forgone) | −20 | −70 | **16 − S**; reaches zero at any S ≥ 16 |

**What this shows.**

- R3's targets hold: Paradox play ends at 30 or below, and Agreement play at 40–50.
- But at the `S` the Agreement path needs, a player who takes every Paradox route **goes Hollow
  before the chapter ends**. The same is true of anyone who skips most acts of Agreement, whatever
  the Paradox costs are. `S` is the lever, not these costs.
- Hollow is a playable state (ruling 2, A3), so this is legal. It is a strong outcome, so it is
  question 4 below.

## 5. What implementation needs (after approval)

The Codex lane, roughly 1–2 sessions:

1. **`quests/dom_side_quest.gd`:** add `PARADOX_TAG := "paradox"`. `has_valid_reward_schema()`
   accepts a negative delta exactly when the outcome is tagged `paradox`, and a positive delta exactly
   when it is tagged `act_of_agreement`, never both. A Paradox outcome may not carry
   `restores_agreement`.
2. **`QuestRegistry.resolve_side_quest()`:** apply a Paradox delta through
   `set_soul_meter_with_cause()`, with cause `quest:<id>:<outcome>`. Skip the Reputation write when
   the outcome names no faction, per question 3.
3. **The chip.** `ui/dialogue/dialogue_choice_info.gd` derives chips from a response's own `do`
   lines, so the row and the ledger agree. Teach `effect_of()` to read the soul delta that
   `QuestRegistry.resolve_side_quest()`, `resolve_broken_muster()` and `resolve_unanswered_roar()`
   will apply, from the quest resource or the ruling table: one source, no copy in the dialogue. The
   Deep Trial's inline options get an explicit `set_soul_meter_with_cause()` line, and their chips
   derive as they do today.
4. **Dialogue:** one response per quest hub after the two outcome responses, plus one read-back
   response. No new `#tag`: SR3 says "a normal option". Regenerate the POT.
5. **Evidence:** `tools/quest_audit.gd` reports every Paradox outcome with a visible cost equal to
   its logged delta, within −4..−10. The chapter-journey harness runs the three paths in §4. This is
   #594's acceptance check.
